Philosophy

The character of the place,
and the people behind it.

A good restaurant exists for reasons you can only find in that place: the plasterer who finished the earthen walls, the carpenter who planed the cypress counter, the sommelier who chooses the wine.

We start by listening to those stories, then choose the photographs and words one by one, so the atmosphere comes through in both Japanese and English.

The place
