CUISINE & WINE

Cuisine & Wine

French technique, Japanese ingredients, cooked simply so they taste their best. The star is brand wagyu steak, with fish, seasonal starters and rice from the kamado — for special occasions and for any day.

01

Starters

Seasonal vegetables in small dishes, a little at a time. The course begins slowly, with the first glass of wine.

02

Fish

Fish, much of it from Mie, is seared until the skin is crisp and served with a sauce of seasonal vegetables.

03

Brand wagyu steak

Matsusaka beef from Mie, Maezawa beef from Iwate, Manyo beef from Tottori — we buy whichever brand wagyu is best that season and grill it over charcoal. Each steak is rested, then carved.

The brand served depends on the day's supply.

04

Rice from the kamado

The meal ends with rice cooked in a kamado, a traditional clay stove finished by a master plasterer. Depending on the season, it may be scented with truffle.to confirm

WINE

Centred on Burgundy

Our wines are chosen with Yuka Miyama of Yuka Selections, an importer devoted to Burgundy and Champagne. Because she deals directly with the growers, our list includes Burgundies that are hard to find elsewhere.

By the glass or by the bottle, matched to the food. Soft drinks are available for guests who don't drink.pairing / non-alcoholic options to confirm

WINE PARTNER

Wine selection partner: Yuka Miyama

Founder, Yuka Selections

After ten years at a major global consulting firm, she moved to France to pursue wine. She lived in Burgundy for four years, earning diplomas from the national agricultural college and the University of Burgundy and training at local wineries. Having visited more than 300 estates, she worked as a tour planner, interpreter and lecturer, and founded the wine importer Yuka Selections in 2020.

A message from Yuka Miyama

Yuka Selections is a very small importer devoted to Burgundy and Champagne, known for wines where “everything tastes good”. We work only with small producers highly regarded by Michelin-starred restaurants and professionals across Europe, and keep every bottle at around 16 °C from the cellar to your table, so the quality stays as close as possible to how it tastes in France. Made from naturally farmed grapes in clean cellars, these wines are full of pure fruit and slip down with graceful ease — we hope you enjoy them.
