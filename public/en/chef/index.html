THE CHEF

Junya NakanishiOWNER-CHEF

Drawing on everything French cooking has taught me, I want to make food that is simply delicious.

Food for a special occasion, of course — but also food you'd want to eat again on an ordinary day.

Not “classic French”, but dishes made with Japanese ingredients that are, quite simply, good.
