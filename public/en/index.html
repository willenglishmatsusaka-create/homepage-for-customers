CONCEPT

Good food, and a blissful moment.

Matsusaka beef from Mie, Maezawa beef from Iwate, Manyo beef from Tottori, and seasonal vegetables. We work closely with our producers and their ingredients, and shape their care into each plate.

Wines chosen by our sommelier and chef, and time to savour every course.

We hope the time you spend here becomes a memory you keep. We look forward to welcoming you in Kyoto — each guest a connection we treasure.

Junya Nakanishi, owner-chef
