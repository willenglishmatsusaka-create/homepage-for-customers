THE CHEF

Junya Nakanishi

“I work closely with our producers and their ingredients, and shape their care into each plate. I hope the time you spend here becomes a memory you keep.”

Born in Kyoto, Nakanishi began his career at Chihana, a kaiseki restaurant in Gion, before turning to Western cuisine. In 2002 he moved to New York to cook at Alain Ducasse at the Essex House.

Back in Japan he worked at Beige Alain Ducasse Tokyo, Mandarin Oriental Tokyo and Pierre Gagnaire at Mandarin Oriental Hong Kong, then served as head chef at Armani Caffè, Ueno Fugetsudo and Aman Tokyo. In 2026 he returned to his home city to open BIEN MANGER.

MANYO BEEF

Purebred Tajima wagyu

Manyo beef comes only from the pure Tajima bloodline — the same lineage behind Kobe beef — raised in small numbers by dedicated farmers in Inaba, Tottori. Delicate yet deep in flavour, its fat melts cleanly and leaves a long, elegant finish. BIEN MANGER is a certified Manyo beef restaurant (No. 119).

Each cut is cooked differently, then rested before it is sliced. From the counter you can watch that final moment up close.
